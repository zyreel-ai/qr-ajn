QUICK ANSWERS

QR AJN FAQ

Simple answers about QR codes, short links, accounts and analytics.

Is QR AJN free?

QR AJN provides free QR creation and free short links. Static QR creation does not require an account.

Do I need to login or sign up?

No. You can create QR codes and short links without login or signup. Dynamic items use a private management link for later edits.

Are static QR codes unlimited?

QR AJN does not place a creation counter on static QR codes generated in your browser. Server-backed features remain protected by fair-use and anti-abuse limits.

Are short-link clicks tracked live?

Yes. Supported short links collect real click analytics and the open analytics view refreshes automatically, typically every five seconds while visible.
