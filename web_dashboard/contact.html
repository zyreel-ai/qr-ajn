What to include

When reporting a problem, include the QR AJN version if visible, the browser used, the page or feature involved, and the exact visible error message.

Keep management links private

Do not share private management URLs, recovery files or management tokens in public issue reports, screenshots or social posts.

The public website should display only official support contact details. This page intentionally does not invent a physical office address.
