Privacy

QR AJN is designed to collect only the information needed to provide QR redirects, public profiles, shared documents and basic analytics.

Public profile information

Information entered into a public profile — such as a name, business description, phone number, email address, website, social links, address, images and PDFs — is intentionally public when the profile is active. Only publish information and documents you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Analytics information

Trackable QR and profile analytics may record event time, broad device type, browser, operating system, referrer and an approximate visitor identifier. The local implementation derives the identifier with a keyed one-way hash and does not expose raw IP addresses in the analytics interface.

Private management links

There is no account system in this edition. A private management link controls editing, analytics and deletion. Anyone who receives that link may be able to manage the associated QR or profile, so keep it private.

Uploaded files

Public profile images and PDF documents are stored by the application and are accessible when attached to an active public profile. Deleting a profile removes its local uploaded files in this implementation.

Local testing

The downloadable localhost package stores its database and uploaded files on the computer running QR AJN. A future public deployment must use durable storage appropriate for the selected hosting environment.
