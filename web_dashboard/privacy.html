Privacy Policy

QR AJN is designed to collect only the information needed to provide supported redirects, public profiles, shared documents and analytics.

CONTENTSPublic informationAnalyticsManagement linksUploaded filesLocal testing

Public profile information

Information entered into a public profile — such as a name, business description, phone number, email address, website, social links, address, images and PDFs — is intentionally public when the profile is active. Only publish information and documents you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Analytics information

Trackable QR and profile analytics may record event time, broad device type, browser, operating system, referrer and an approximate visitor identifier. The implementation derives the identifier with a keyed one-way hash and does not expose raw IP addresses in the analytics interface.

Analytics begin with real events. QR AJN does not populate the production analytics interface with fake traffic.

Private management links

There is no account system in this edition. A private management link controls supported editing, analytics and deletion. Anyone who receives that link may be able to manage the associated item, so keep it private.

Uploaded files

Public profile images and PDF documents are stored by the application and are accessible when attached to an active public resource. Only upload files you are authorized to publish.

Local testing

The downloadable localhost package can store its database and uploaded files on the computer running QR AJN. Production persistence depends on the configured durable storage services.
