About QR AJN

QR AJN is a simple web tool for creating QR codes and public profile pages. A public profile can use a clean address such as qrajn.online/yourname, share contact information and public PDF documents, and provide scan, page-view and click analytics through a private management link.

Simple by design

This edition does not require accounts, workspaces, premium plans or billing. The private management URL created with a trackable QR or public profile acts as the management key, so it should be kept private.

Analytics

Trackable QR scans and public profile activity can record approximate device, browser and operating-system information. QR AJN uses a one-way visitor hash for approximate unique-visitor counts and does not display raw IP addresses in analytics.
