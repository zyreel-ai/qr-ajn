# QR AJN V8.4 Scope

## Included
Professional QR creation/customization, trackable QR redirects, scan-quality guidance, QR templates and frames, public no-account profiles, profile themes/templates, PDF sharing, private management links, real-time scan/view/action analytics, 30-day trend data, responsive UI, accessibility/reduced motion, security validation and serialized local persistence.

## Explicitly excluded
Authentication, signup/login, account/workspace, pricing, premium, billing, Razorpay, Google Cloud Functions and automatic deployment.

## Trust badge
The blue check displayed in the UI says **Published**. It is a publication/status indicator only and does not claim identity or business verification.
