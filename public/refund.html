Refund Policy

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 21 August 2026

Billing and refunds depend on the payment provider and the plan purchased. Duplicate charges and verified billing errors will be investigated. Subscription cancellation stops future renewals according to provider timing; previously consumed service periods are normally not refundable unless required by applicable law.

Failed payments

QR AJN can place accounts into a grace or past-due state before plan downgrade. Data is not immediately destroyed because a payment fails.
