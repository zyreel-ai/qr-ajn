QR AJN

Advertising Policy

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 21 August 2026

All paid placements must be clearly labeled Sponsored. Advertisers must accurately represent the promoted profile, offer and destination.

Not accepted

QR AJN does not accept campaigns that facilitate unlawful goods or services, malware, phishing, fraudulent claims, deceptive impersonation, hate or extremist promotion, exploitative sexual content, illegal drugs, prohibited weapons, or other content that violates applicable law or platform policy.

Targeting

Campaign targeting is limited to supported contextual and coarse geographic signals. Advertisers must not attempt to infer sensitive personal characteristics from scanner data.
