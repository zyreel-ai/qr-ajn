QR AJN

Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 21 August 2026

QR AJN stores account, profile, QR, lead and analytics data needed to operate the service. Public QR analytics use privacy-conscious identifiers and coarse location signals; raw IP addresses are not intentionally stored in QR AJN analytics records.

Scanner data

Anonymous scanning does not reveal a person's real name, phone number or email. Contact details are collected only when the scanner explicitly submits a consent-based form or signs in.

Data rights

Account owners can export their QR AJN data and schedule recoverable account deletion. Deleted profiles and QR records use a recovery period before permanent purge.

Advertising and cookies

Optional advertising or analytics scripts must respect the user's consent choice where required. Necessary storage is used for sign-in, security and application functionality.

Security

QR AJN uses HTTPS, access controls, audit records and provider-managed infrastructure. No system can guarantee absolute security.
